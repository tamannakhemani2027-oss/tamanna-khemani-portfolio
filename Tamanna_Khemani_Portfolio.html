About

Trained in customer psychology before I ever studied marketing.

I spent two years at Godrej Properties sitting inside the messiest part of a customer's journey — the follow-up calls, the escalations, the moment someone almost cancels. That vantage point taught me something most marketing case studies skip: retention and acquisition are the same discipline, just pointed in different directions.

I'm now at Masters' Union studying technology and business, and using the time to build the things I used to only analyse — a live AI tool that maps who actually influences a purchase, a dropshipping brand that crossed ₹5L in two months, and brand campaigns for a D2C gifting label that sold out events and stopped scrolls. Before any of that, fourteen years of classical Bharatanatyam and a diploma in fine arts taught me how to hold an audience's attention on purpose — a skill that turns out to matter as much in a campaign brief as it does on a stage.
